Basic Makeup Steps

  • Prepare and moisturize your skin.
  • Apply foundation or another light base product.
  • Add blush to bring color to the face.
  • Define the eyes with shadow, eyeliner or mascara.
  • Finish the look with lipstick, gloss or lip tint.

Makeup Tip

Start with a small amount of product and build it slowly. Blending carefully usually gives a cleaner and more natural result.

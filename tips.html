Makeup Tips

Simple beauty tips that can make your makeup look cleaner and more natural.

Everyday Makeup Tips

  1. Clean and moisturize your skin before applying makeup.
  2. Apply foundation in thin layers instead of using too much product.
  3. Blend blush carefully so there are no harsh lines.
  4. Use mascara from the roots of the lashes to the tips.
  5. Choose a lipstick shade that matches the mood of your look.

Things to Avoid

  • Do not apply makeup on dirty or very dry skin.
  • Do not use too much foundation or concealer at once.
  • Do not forget to blend the edges of your makeup.
  • Do not share eye or lip products with other people.
  • Do not sleep with makeup on.

Easy Routine

  • Prepare your skin.
  • Create an even base.
  • Add blush or bronzer.
  • Define your eyes.
  • Finish with a lip product.

Remember

Good makeup is not about using as many products as possible. Clean application, careful blending and colors that suit you usually create the best result.
